The Vision

TO THINK BEYOND SOLAR ENERGY

The Vision is an energy museum design project by Tejas Ajit Jamdar, Gauri Thange and Ansh Agarwal. The proposal takes “To think beyond solar energy” as its guiding theme, placing energy at the centre of the museum’s architectural story.

The project was recognised as second runner-up in the West Zone. Its original presentation sheets are collected below, with a three-dimensional model available through the interactive portfolio.
